# Hook library

55 hook formulas with examples in wes's voice. A hook is the first line. It carries the event or the number, never the setup, never the product name. Pick the formula the story supports. If the real story is smaller than the hook wants, shrink the hook.

Voice rules for every example: short sentences, contractions, no em dashes, no semicolons, capitalized starts, understated excitement. Never engagement bait.

## The Receipt (number first)

1. `[Number] [bad thing] in [timeframe].`
   A background plugin burned $227.72 in 8 days.
2. `I [did X]. It cost [number].`
   I delegated a one-line edit. It cost 77k tokens.
3. `[Number] [things] and [timeframe] later, [result].`
   3.7M tokens and two hours later, I closed it by hand in 40 minutes.
4. `We replaced a $[big]/yr [thing] with a $[small]/mo [thing].`
   We replaced a $25k/yr contract with a $20/mo tool.
5. `[Tool] did [surprising thing] [when].`
   My agent bought a domain at 2am and started building.
6. `[Number] [units], [number] [other units].`
   19 hooks, 36 skills, 5 subagents. None of them were planned.
7. `It took [number] [attempts] to learn [lesson].`
   It took 16,561 API calls to learn that background plugins need spend ceilings.
8. `[Percentage]% of [thing] is [surprising truth].`
   90% of my agent's token bill is context it never uses.
